## Design Context

### Users

The product has one owner who self-hosts it and opens the same private reading library from desktop and mobile devices. The primary job is sustained RSS and saved-article reading, with personal state such as favorites, read status, history, notes, and AI-assisted reading kept consistent across devices.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, focused, and restrained. The interface should feel like a dependable personal reading instrument rather than a social product, content portal, or infrastructure dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing QMReader visual language: neutral black-and-white surfaces, compact controls, low-radius geometry, strong typography, and light/dark theme awareness. Login should be a sparse editorial threshold into the reading workspace, not a marketing page, decorative hero, or generic SaaS card.

### Design Principles

1. Put reading continuity ahead of product explanation or decoration.
2. Keep owner-only controls direct, compact, and easy to scan.
3. Preserve the existing neutral palette and spacing rhythm across new surfaces.
4. Make authentication states explicit and accessible without exposing system detail.
5. Adapt critical workflows for narrow screens instead of hiding them.
